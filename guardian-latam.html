Guardian Prompt Library

AI prompts built
for partner SEs

Paste these into your AI of choice to accelerate conversations, build expansion cases, and prepare for technical challenges. Updated by the Guardian program team.

Deal strategy
Build the VM-to-EM expansion case
"My customer [Company] recently renewed their Tenable VM license. They have [X] assets, primarily on-prem with some cloud. Help me build the business case for expanding to Tenable One — including how to frame the ROI conversation and what discovery questions will reveal their exposure gaps."
POC preparation
Design a POC success criteria document
"I'm running a 30-day Tenable One POC with [Customer]. Their key concern is [concern]. Draft a POC success criteria document with 5 measurable outcomes, a week-by-week activity plan, and suggested stakeholder check-ins."
Technical positioning
Handle the "we already have a scanner" objection
"A prospect says they already use [Competitor] and don't see why they'd switch. Help me build a technical differentiation talking track that focuses on breadth of coverage (IT, OT, cloud, identity) and business risk context, not just feature comparisons."
Discovery
OT/ICS environment discovery questions
"I have a meeting with the OT/ICS security lead at a manufacturing company. Give me 10 discovery questions that will surface their current visibility gaps, regulatory pressure (NIS2, IEC 62443, LGPD), and why agent-based scanning is impractical in their environment."
RFP / Proposal
Respond to an exposure management RFP section
"I'm responding to an RFP that asks: '[paste question]'. The customer is in [industry]. Using Tenable's positioning around Exposure Management and Tenable One, draft a compelling 200-word technical response that addresses the question and highlights our differentiation."
Exec conversations
Translate a scan report into a CISO briefing
"I have Tenable scan results showing [X critical, Y high vulns] across [Z] assets. My audience is a CISO who doesn't want technical detail. Write a 5-minute verbal briefing that translates these findings into business risk language, with 3 prioritized actions."
